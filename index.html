Wealth Circuit Publisher

A publishing tool for creators who run more than one TikTok channel. Connect each of your own accounts once, then post each video to the right account — from one place, through TikTok's official API.

What it does

One login per account. Each TikTok account is connected once via TikTok Login Kit. You approve exactly which account the tool may post to — nothing else.
Post to the right channel. Pick a finished video file and the account it belongs to. The tool uploads it with the Content Posting API as a public video, with your chosen caption, comments on, and duet/stitch off.
Your content, your accounts. The tool only ever posts videos you made, to TikTok accounts you authorized. It never re-uploads other creators' content and adds no watermark.

How your data is handled

When you connect an account, TikTok issues the tool an access token and a refresh token. Tokens are stored securely on the tool's own server, used only to publish the videos you choose, and refreshed automatically. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings — the tool then loses all access immediately. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
