Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What this tool is

Wealth Circuit Publisher is a publishing tool for creators who run more than one TikTok channel. It posts videos you made to TikTok accounts you authorized, using TikTok's official API.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account identifiers (open_id and nickname) for each account you connect — used to show you which account a video will be posted to.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok when you connect an account — used only to upload and publish the videos you choose.
  • Video files and captions you provide — transmitted to TikTok at the moment you post; we do not keep copies longer than needed to complete the upload.

How we use it

Your data is used for one purpose only: publishing the videos you choose to the TikTok accounts you authorized. Tokens are stored securely on our own server, never logged, and refreshed automatically so you don't have to reconnect.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not post anything you didn't choose to post.
  • We do not access TikTok accounts you haven't connected.
  • We use no advertising trackers on this site.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in your TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately. To request deletion of any stored data, email joingood124@gmail.com.
