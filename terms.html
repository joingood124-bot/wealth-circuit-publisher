You are responsible for the content you publish and for keeping your TikTok account credentials safe. Connecting an account grants the tool permission to post videos to that account until you revoke it.

The tool is provided as-is. Posting depends on TikTok's API availability and on your app's review status with TikTok; we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service.

Termination
